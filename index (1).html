Ezinne Esther Kalu-Egbe’s story is one of family, faith, hard work and service. As a mother and grandmother, she helped shape the lives of those closest to her. As a woman of Christian faith, she gave her time and commitment to the church, including evangelism and leadership in the women’s fellowship.

She was also known as an industrious businesswoman and a generous presence in her community. Her legacy is found not only in the milestones of her life, but in the encouragement she offered, the people she helped and the values she passed on.
